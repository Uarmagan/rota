# Security policy

Please do not report vulnerabilities in a public issue.

Email `ugurarmagan93@gmail.com` with the subject `Rota security`. Include a
short description, the affected area, impact, and minimal reproduction steps.
Do not access or retain data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue.

Rota is a best-effort open-source demo. There is no guaranteed response time,
support commitment, or bug bounty. Reports will be reviewed and addressed when
practical.
